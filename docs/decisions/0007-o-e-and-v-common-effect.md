# 0007: O − E and V data stay common-effect only, as in RevMan

Decided by the owner, 2026-11-18 (issue #100). Recorded in rule R25.

## Decision

The map keeps the "Observed minus expected and variance" method (O − E and V)
common-effect only. No pathway changes. Rule R25 now says this is the limit
RevMan has, which the map follows, rather than a rule of method.

## Why, and what the source says

- **Cochrane Handbook 10.9** says that only fixed-effect methods are available
  in RevMan for "O – E and Variance" outcomes. It states this as a limit of
  the software. It doesn't say that random effects are wrong for these data.
- R25 used to read "fixed-effect methods only", as if it were a methodological
  rule. It now names RevMan, and the "Why not?" reason for O − E and V with
  random effects starts "In this map, ...", as for other choices the map makes
  itself (decision 0004).

## Changing it

Offering random effects for O − E and V would add pathways. It needs a
primary source for a random-effects method on these data and its R
implementation, then an audited map change.
